## Accounts and where to book

## Rate type

Non-refundable by default. Take the flexible rate only when it costs less than £10 more.

## Guests

1 adult, 1 room, unless stated.

## Quality

- **Score bands (Booking.com):** 9.0+ a strong plus; 8.5–8.9 the normal bar; 8.0–8.4 only when much cheaper, labelled.
- **Property type:** hotels only.

## Must-haves

- A desk you can work at.
- Air conditioning.

## Quiet

- **Threshold:** 7.5% of reviews with text complaining of noise (`--threshold 0.075`): flags roughly the noisiest 40% of London hotels rated 8+. Light sleepers might use 6.5% (flags about 60%); heavy sleepers 8.5% (about 30%).
- **Hard disqualifiers:** in the last 12 months, a guest who moved rooms or left because of noise.
- **Building type:** prefer purpose-built hotels over converted old houses at similar noise results.

## Strong pluses

- A gym (best with free weights).

## Room taste

Design-led rooms with character: warm natural materials, considered colour, good lighting and curated art, in a characterful building or after a careful refurbishment. Avoid the grey corporate business-hotel look and budget-chain rooms.

## Price

No fixed ceiling: around, or a little above, the going rate for a well-rated hotel in that area. Lower is better at equal quality.

## Area rules

- **City stays:** central, or within walking distance of my targets.
- **Airport stopovers:** in-terminal or walking distance best; a short shuttle is acceptable.
